Yasik Shorts Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This application accesses only the OAuth access token issued for the Yasik accounts, used solely to publish video content those accounts own.

Data accessed

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the authorized Yasik accounts only.
  • Video files and metadata supplied by the App's own operator for publishing.

Data not collected

No data from any other user or account is collected, stored, or shared with third parties.

Data retention

Tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the App and are revocable at any time by the account owner.
