Yasik Shorts Publisher — Terms of Service

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This application ("the App") publishes short-form video content to the Yasik (@yasik.news / @yasik_news) accounts via TikTok's Content Posting API and YouTube's Data API v3.

What this App does

  • Uploads and publishes video content on behalf of the Yasik accounts only.
  • Does not access, act on behalf of, or publish to any other account.
  • Every publish is reviewed by a human operator before it goes live.

What this App does not do

  • It does not collect data from other users.
  • It does not sell, share, or monetize any user data.
